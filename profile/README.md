# OpenFinMind

### Open Financial Intelligence

**An Open Research Community for AI in Finance**

OpenFinMind is an open research community at **The University of Hong Kong (HKU)**, advancing artificial intelligence in finance through open research, collaborative development, and shared resources.

## Research Areas

- **AI Agents & Financial Research** — Intelligent agents, multi-agent systems, and deep research for financial analysis and decision-making.
- **Quantitative Finance & Alpha Discovery** — Code-based alpha mining, factor discovery, and systematic investment.
- **Financial Forecasting & Evaluation** — Financial prediction, temporal evaluation, and AI benchmarks.
- **Financial Data & Research Infrastructure** — Open datasets, reproducible workflows, and research tools.

## Selected Research

- **CogAlpha** — An LLM-driven framework combining financial reasoning and code-based evolutionary search for automated alpha discovery. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.538/) · [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.538.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenFinMind/CogAlpha_Prompt) · ACL 2026.

- **X-Alpha** — A memory-driven AI Quant Researcher that transforms financial knowledge and accumulated research experience into continuous hypothesis-to-code alpha discovery. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08332) · arXiv:2607.08332.

- **FinForesight** — A diagnostic benchmark evaluating the financial forecasting capabilities of deep research systems across CN, US, and HK markets through four forecasting tasks and two diagnostic tasks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01621) · [Code & Benchmark](https://github.com/OpenFinMind/FinForesight) · arXiv:2611.01621.
