# OpenFinMind

**Open Financial Intelligence**

OpenFinMind is an open research community at The University of Hong Kong dedicated to advancing AI for Finance.

We bring together researchers, developers, and practitioners to explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, quantitative finance, and financial research.

Our community builds, shares, and connects open-source projects across:

- **AI Agents:** Intelligent agents and autonomous financial research systems.
- **Quantitative Finance:** Quantitative strategies, factor discovery, and algorithmic trading.
- **Financial Intelligence:** Financial reasoning, forecasting, and deep research.
- **Models & Datasets:** Open models, financial datasets, and research tools.
- **Benchmarks & Evaluation:** Reproducible experiments and rigorous evaluation frameworks.

We believe in open knowledge, collaborative innovation, and cumulative research.

**Build. Share. Discover. Advance.**

Together, we build the future of Financial Intelligence.
